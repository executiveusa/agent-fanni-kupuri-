# Agent Fanny End-to-End Workflow Map

The uploaded packages are source material, not runtime skills. Fanny uses them as stage procedures inside complete business workflows.

## Workflow A — Media Intelligence to Executive Report

1. **Discover** — ingest approved exports and normalize sources.
2. **Protect** — redact sensitive fields and hash source references.
3. **Classify** — topic, dimension, relevance, sentiment, and risk.
4. **Verify** — compare rules, evidence, duplicates, and confidence.
5. **Resolve** — route uncertainty and high-risk records to the verifier policy.
6. **Synthesize** — identify narratives, changes, and anomalies.
7. **Report** — produce an executive report and evidence package.
8. **Measure** — calculate time saved, correction rate, and report quality.
9. **Productize** — update reusable taxonomy and pilot proof without copying client data.

## Workflow B — Strategy to Social Publishing

Source packages: social-media-skills, Facebook automation, Hermes Facebook, Postiz-compatible operations.

1. **Business context** — offer, customer, objective, proof, constraints.
2. **Audience evidence** — audience signals, pains, language, and buying context.
3. **Content strategy** — themes, platform roles, funnel stages, and measurement plan.
4. **Pattern analysis** — learn from prior approved content and performance.
5. **Calendar** — define cadence, campaign sequence, and production requirements.
6. **Asset production** — hooks, posts, carousels, threads, captions, and repurposed variants.
7. **Claims and rights check** — validate sources, permissions, and prohibited claims.
8. **Approval policy** — automated approval only for low-risk pre-approved content classes.
9. **Schedule/publish** — Postiz or platform connector, idempotency key, audit record, rollback plan.
10. **Monitor** — delivery status, comments, failures, and emerging risk.
11. **Analyze** — reach, engagement, conversion, cost, and audience growth.
12. **Optimize** — next-best action chosen from measured evidence.
13. **Report and learn** — commercial result, lessons, and workflow revision.

## Workflow C — Meta Ads Profit Loop

1. Confirm commercial objective and unit economics.
2. Audit account and tracking integrity.
3. Establish 7/14/30-day baseline.
4. Diagnose campaign, ad-set, audience, placement, and creative performance.
5. Produce a hypothesis register with knowns and unknowns.
6. Rank actions by expected profit impact, risk, reversibility, and evidence.
7. Build a bounded experiment with spend and stop limits.
8. Require explicit budget authority before writes.
9. Monitor CPA, ROAS, CTR, frequency, conversion quality, and spend.
10. Stop, continue, or scale according to pre-approved rules.
11. Produce a profit and learning report.

## Workflow D — Voice to Action

Source: Oracle SpeakFlow.

1. Capture locally with visible microphone state.
2. Transcribe using local Whisper by default.
3. Apply Kupuri dictionary and bilingual normalization.
4. Detect intent and target workflow.
5. Restate the requested outcome and constraints.
6. Create a workflow run and input manifest.
7. Execute or delegate the current stage.
8. Speak the result, evidence, risk, and next action.
9. Preserve transcript according to workspace retention policy.

## Workflow E — Client Discovery to Deployable Agent

Source: Kupuri Agent Alex ICM package.

1. Discover approved sources.
2. Conduct structured interview.
3. Build the business model and measurable offer.
4. Define voice and brand system.
5. Configure social operations.
6. Define permissions and privacy.
7. Validate contradictions and incomplete decisions.
8. Compile a constructor manifest.
9. Deploy an isolated workspace.
10. Measure outcomes and productize reusable mechanisms.

## Autonomy policy

Fanny does not require a human inside routine, pre-approved stages. Human approval is required only at explicit authority boundaries: new data classes, credentials, spending, legal claims, external publishing without prior policy, destructive operations, and production release. The goal is zero unnecessary human handling—not zero governance.
